How to read this explorer

  • Last author is usually the senior researcher who leads the work. First author usually did most of the work.
  • Gender is inferred from first names with Gender-API (96.4% of authorships classified). It is binary and may be wrong for some names, especially East Asian names. It does not record how people identify. The share of women last authors stays between 17.7% and 21.8% across four checks.
  • “From that country” means the author's address is in a country the paper names. African-led means the first or last author has an African address.
  • Funding comes from Web of Science acknowledgements, which are indexed well only from 2008. That is why the data start in 2008.
  • WHO priorities are tagged with a keyword list. This is transparent but approximate, and one paper can match several priorities.
  • Small groups give unstable numbers. When fewer than 10 papers match your filters, we show the count but not a percentage.
  • These are patterns, not causes. The data stop in February 2023, before the outbreaks in Equatorial Guinea, Tanzania, Rwanda and Ethiopia.
